## 🛠️ Video Recording & Editing Tips (Top Quality Checklist)

1. **Screen Resolution:** Record at `1920x1080 (1080p 60fps)` using OBS Studio or Chrome Screen Recorder.
2. **Audio:** Use a good mic with noise suppression (e.g. Krisp or Audacity).
3. **Background Music:** Use an uplifting, subtle corporate tech / ambient electronic track at `-22dB` volume so your voiceover is 100% crisp and clear.
4. **Mouse Cursor:** Enable cursor highlight or smooth zoom effects (CapCut, Premiere Pro, or DaVinci Resolve) during button clicks.
5. **Captions:** Add bold word-by-word subtitles (CapCut Auto-Captions) — hackathon judges love watching demos with subtitles even on mute!
